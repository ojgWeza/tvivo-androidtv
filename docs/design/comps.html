I  App mark — the pulse, not a letter
Tvivo is TV + vivo (vivre: alive, quick, vivid), so the mark is a live signal inside a screen, not a monogram. The beat cuts a sharp V trough, so the V of vivo falls out of the pulse instead of being spelled.
